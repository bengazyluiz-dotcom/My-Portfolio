Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions?

Here are answers to common questions about project timelines and workflows.

How long does a typical project take?
Timeline depends on project scope. Brand identity packages usually take 1–2 weeks, web development projects take 2–3 weeks, and video editing turnarounds range from 3–7 business days.
Can you handle all 3 disciplines (Design, Web & Video) for one project?
Yes! That is my core specialization. You get unified brand strategy, modern code, and video promos delivered by a single point of contact without losing quality or design consistency.
How do payment and contract milestones work?
Standard engagements require a 50% deposit upfront before work commences, with the remaining 50% due upon final review and approval before final asset handoff.
Do you work with international remote clients?
Absolutely. I work remotely with clients across North America, Europe, and Africa utilizing Figma, GitHub, Google Drive, and WhatsApp for seamless collaboration.
